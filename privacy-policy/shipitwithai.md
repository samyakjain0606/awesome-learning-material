# Privacy Policy — shipitwithai

Last updated: October 5, 2026

shipitwithai ("the app") is a private automation used only by the owner of the Instagram account @shipitwithai. It replies to people who comment on @shipitwithai's posts and reels.

## What data the app accesses

When someone comments on a post or reel published by @shipitwithai, the app reads, through the official Instagram API:

- the comment text and its ID
- the commenter's Instagram username
- the time of the comment

## How the data is used

The app checks whether the comment contains a keyword (for example "LINK"). If it does, it sends the commenter one direct message through Instagram's private reply feature. It may also post a short public reply to the comment. The data is used for nothing else.

## Storage and retention

The app stores only the IDs of comments it has already handled, so that nobody receives the same message twice. These records are kept on the owner's computer or server and are deleted automatically after 8 days. Comment text and usernames are not stored permanently.

## Sharing

The data is not sold, rented or shared with any third party, and it is not used for advertising.

## Deleting your data

To have any data about you deleted, send a direct message to @shipitwithai on Instagram or email samyakjain0606@yahoo.com. It will be deleted within 7 days.
